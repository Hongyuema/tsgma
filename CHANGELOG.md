# Release history

- Require cumulative support across crude, adjusted and temporal stages, with agreement in direction.
- Use all three unadjusted P values in the third-star conjunction, followed by full-family Holm correction.
- Update primary grades to 29 zero-star, 12 one-star, seven two-star and no three-star pairs.

The numerical reconstruction reproduced 116 tables and four metadata records; eight independent tests passed.
