## Health outcomes

Source: World Health Organization, Global Health Observatory; non-HDL cholesterol, raised fasting blood glucose and hypertension estimates, processed by Our World in Data. The frozen OWID release is dated 22 May 2026 and its source metadata identify the contributing studies. Underlying observations were supplied by the countries and populations represented in those studies. The separate frozen WHO diabetes query is used as an independent source check.

The included full indicator metadata label the WHO source CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and mark it as redistributable. These records are retained. The WHO copyright page describes CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO for WHO publications; its older data-compilation terms permit distribution for public-health purposes. The newer data.who.int portal uses different terms. This archive does not treat those newer terms as a blanket relicensing of the frozen GHO inputs. Reuse is for non-commercial research with source attribution and the applicable share-alike conditions. No WHO endorsement is implied.

## Study processing and limitations

The analysis aligns country codes and observation years, aggregates food items, constructs complete-case samples, calculates country means and delayed variables, and estimates the models described in the code. The historical workbook also contains tobacco, overweight and recorded-calorie covariates whose exact source vintages or construction are incompletely documented. Those limitations are retained; the values are not presented as newly verified original measurements.

Our World in Data's own processing and documentation are available under CC BY, while third-party data retain their provider terms: https://ourworldindata.org/faqs#can-i-reuse-or-republish-your-data

Publication artwork, BioRender illustrations, Illustrator artboards, native Prism layouts and published source PDFs are excluded. Numerical observations exported from the study's Prism projects are included for reproducibility. The original sources, not this compilation, remain authoritative for data definitions and reuse conditions. Licence notices were checked on 25 November 2026.
